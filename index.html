The release-engineering lens

This module teaches the machinery. Your job is to use that machinery to move a safe, traceable package of microservices from commit to customer.

Commit→Build & test→Versioned artifacts→Environments→Observe & release
Continuous deliveryEvery change is built, tested, and kept deployable. A human or policy may still approve production.
Continuous deploymentEvery change that passes the automated safeguards is deployed automatically. This needs strong tests, monitoring, rollback, and small changes.
For your platformDecide whether services version and deploy independently or travel as a tested release train. Make the dependency contract explicit.
