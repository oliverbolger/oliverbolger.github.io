Your working context

The complete CI/CD lens

This module teaches the machinery. Your job is to use it to move a safe, traceable package of microservices from code change to customer, with fast feedback throughout.

Integrate→Build & test→Package→Promote→Deploy→Observe
Continuous integrationDevelopers integrate small changes frequently. The pipeline builds, tests, scans, and reports quickly so problems are found before release.
Continuous deliveryEvery passing change is kept deployable. A human or policy may still approve production, but the package is ready to go.
Continuous deploymentEvery change that passes the automated safeguards is deployed automatically. This needs strong tests, monitoring, rollback, and small changes.
